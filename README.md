### 4️⃣ Statistical Validation

## Hypothesis Testing

### Null Hypothesis (H0)
There is no significant difference in sales between regions.

### Alternative Hypothesis (H1)
West region has significantly higher sales than other regions.

### Test Used
- Independent T-Test

### Result
- p-value < 0.05
- Result is statistically significant

This indicates that the sales difference is statistically meaningful.

## 📈 Key Insights

### 💻 Technology Category Leads Sales
Technology products generated the highest revenue among all categories.

### 🌍 West Region Performs Best
The West region contributed the highest overall sales.

### 📅 Seasonal Sales Trends
Sales showed fluctuations across months, indicating seasonal patterns.

### 🛒 Top Products Drive Revenue
A few high-performing sub-categories significantly contributed to total sales.

### 🔗 Quantity Impacts Revenue
Higher quantities sold directly increased total sales.
